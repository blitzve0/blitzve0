## The work

My Instagram practice brings together cars, training, music and travel. The work is more than a grid: it includes capture choices, editing, audio and the decisions around sharing. I have started keeping dated records of the account and individual posts so the next creative choice has evidence behind it. Two account snapshots show a change from 180 followers on 19 September to 2,496 on 9 October 2026, with paid promotion included in distribution. I want to present that honestly, alongside actual creative outputs and the process. The journey demonstrates persistence and range; it does not establish an organic-only result or an engineering-product performance claim.

## Problem

A public profile can show the finished image while hiding the work around it: capture, editing, audio, posting decisions and reading the results. I wanted the journey to show creative practice and the discipline of learning from measured evidence.

## Decisions

- Keep the personal voice grounded in travel, cars, fitness and music while using engineering work as occasional behind-the-scenes context.
- Use actual source portraits and supplied real-world footage; label conceptual promotional art as artwork.
- Read account counts and per-post metrics in their dated scopes, with paid distribution disclosed and missing values left unknown.

## Evidence and current scope

Dated account snapshots recorded 180 followers on 19 September and 2,496 on 9 October 2026. Paid promotion was part of distribution. The BMW reel recorded 488,217 lifetime All views and 2,135 lifetime All Follows on 9 October; All expressly includes ads. These metrics support a creator journey, not a causal engineering outcome.

## Tradeoffs

Growth signals are context, not proof that one system or creative decision caused the change. Account follower counts and per-post Follows are different measures. The public BMW announcement was edited and posted by me outside Reel OS.
