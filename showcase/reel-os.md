# Reel OS — local media workflow

**The edit should leave evidence.**

**My role:** Builder and editorial owner of a personal media-production system.

**Current stage:** Private local workflow; manual-supervised production with technical QA separate from human review.

## The work

Reel OS turns the edit into something inspectable. Original footage is indexed, selected moments carry exact timecodes, and the editorial plan stays connected to the rendered master. The system produces editable timelines, proof sheets and technical QA so a later session can understand how the cut was made. A supervised phone-to-Mac route uses private Drive for source intake and review delivery. The important product choice is the separation of states: a technically sound render can still need a human verdict, and a published post’s performance must be measured in its own window. That gives the creative process durable records without pretending that a codec check proves a good film.

## Problem

A good short-form edit depends on decisions about source identity, exact moments, pacing, typography and sound. Repeated chat-only editing loses those decisions and makes it difficult to explain or reproduce a result.

## Decisions

- Keep original footage, source inventories and timestamped moment evidence connected to schema-validated edit plans.
- Generate editable OTIO/EDL timelines and deterministic FFmpeg masters with plan-bound preflight and QA.
- Keep technical delivery, normal-speed human review and platform performance as separate records.

## Evidence and current scope

The repository documents a resumable pipeline, shared typography, checksum-bound QA and supervised private Drive intake/delivery. A dated eight-source cycle verified upload, cloud playback and checksum-identical return. The historical contact sheet shows an actual local rendered draft. No automatic analytics connection or performance lift is claimed.

## Tradeoffs

Deterministic checks can verify codecs, title bounds, timecodes and complete decode, but they cannot replace the human judgment of identity, action, story and sound. The BMW announcement was made by me outside Reel OS and is not a tool-generated outcome.
